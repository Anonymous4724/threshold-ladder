Reading the list

Beside each cup, the points the model expects the team at that rank to finish with, and in small type the range nine final thresholds in ten have landed in, measured on past cups. A "qualifies" mark shows the rank the cup's rules send through to the next round.

The line under the forecast says where it comes from: the cup's previous editions in the region when it has some; for a cup that is new in the region, recent leaderboards of the same format replayed under its scoring table, together with its editions in other regions when there are any; for a single-lobby final, the finals of the same format. The guide to new cups explains the replay.

A cup without numbers is one the forecast needs more about – a format with no fixed team size, or no published scoring table. Open it to fill in what is missing.
